## Immutable distinction among result channels

- YOLO11n paired discovery and the build-repeat study are different captures and
  answer different questions.
- CPU source/ONNX application AP, strict raw/numeric diagnostics, and TRT FP16
  feasibility are separate channels. A pass in one cannot erase a FAIL in
  another.
- The proposed v2 study is not yet data. The execution plan, even after it is
  generated, is not a completed result.
- No inference here estimates a new calibration algorithm or proves generality
  beyond the fixed checkpoint/data/runtime design.
